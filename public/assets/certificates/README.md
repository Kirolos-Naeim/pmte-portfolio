# Certificates and compliance assets

Raw certificate scans are intentionally not published in the website asset bundle because the supplied pages contain QR codes, signatures and other sensitive identifiers.

Use `credentials-register.csv` as the sanitised public reference. Before a formal tender or prequalification submission, retrieve the controlled originals from the company records and confirm their validity with the issuing authority.

The ISO and ICV documents supplied in the source portfolio are historical evidence only.
